Save to Cookbo

Safari saves recipes straight into Cookbo’s iCloud folder, so there’s nothing to set up. On a recipe page, click the Cookbo button in the toolbar.

Recipes save as files in Cookbo’s iCloud Drive folder, where the app finds them on all your devices. Chrome needs to be shown that folder once:

  1. Click Choose Folder…
  2. In the sidebar, pick iCloud Drive, then select the Cookbo folder.
  3. Click Select, then allow Chrome to edit files there.
